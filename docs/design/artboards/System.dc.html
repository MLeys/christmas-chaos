Christmas Chaos DESIGN SYSTEM · V2

North Pole Game Show

Deep evergreen night, candy-cane red, tinsel gold and snow cream. Chunky friendly type, a marquee-bulb wheel and big tactile buttons — built to read from the couch and tap with a thumb.

  • Big Screen 1920 × 1080 · read from 3–5 m
  • Phone 390 × 844 · one job per screen
  • Host laptop fluid page · 15–17 px body
