Midnight Premiere · design system

The big screen, assembled

A 1920×1080 TV frame built only from the shared tokens and components below: night sky with stars and margin snow, the top bar with the round pill and bulb turn dots, the bulb string, the marquee wheel resting on Christmas Movies, a silver-screen title card and the frosted score strip. Compare it with the approved DirA-TV board.

01 · wheel.js

The marquee wheel

28 px tinsel rim with a brass bevel, 24 bulbs in a 3-phase chase (1.2 s idle; follows the wheel's speed while spinning; 150 ms hit-stop, then 3 flashes on landing), a 6 px night gutter, snow dividers, Big Shoulders labels, a peppermint hub and a gold teardrop pointer. Double Down gets candy-cane stripes with an ivory pill; Christmas Movies gets a gold curtain-fringe trim.

02 · .btn · .btn-round

Buttons

Three-layer pushable buttons: a blurred shadow, a darker edge and a bright front that rests 4 px up. A press drops it to 2 px in 34 ms; on release it eases back over 600 ms (cubic-bezier(.3,.7,.4,1)). Focus is a 3 px tinsel ring at 3 px. BUZZ is the only big red button.

03 · type

Fraunces · Figtree · Big Shoulders

Fraunces 900 (opsz 144, SOFT 100) for the wordmark, splash words, team names and card titles, and only for static numbers. Figtree for questions, body, buttons and every score and timer (tabular figures). Big Shoulders Display for eyebrows, letterboards, wheel labels and credits.

04 · tokens

Palette

Cool night surfaces; everything you can touch or celebrate is a warm light. Use at most two accents per screen beyond navy, red and gold. Green is only for Correct and chartreuse only for Grinch Steal. Never set cranberry text on navy.

05 · .card · .title-card · .silver-screen

Cards

Solid night-2, radius 28, with a 1 px inner top highlight. Hero cards add a 2 px gold hairline. The title-card frame is a thin double gold rule with art-deco notches. The silver screen (Christmas Movies) is charcoal with a vignette, grain and sprocket holes.

06 · .chip · .stub-chip · .tag · .pill

Chips & tags

Eyebrow chips are slot-colour pills with the icon and a Big Shoulders caps label. Dark slots get a tinsel rim, Christmas Movies wears velvet, and Double Down is an ivory pill with a candy-cane rim. Once a movie is revealed, the film shows as a ticket-stub chip in its own colour.

07 · .ticket

Tickets

ADMIT ONE tickets with notched corners and a perforation. The difficulty tickets get richer foil as they get harder: silver, light gold, then full foil. Gold foil is reserved for the golden ticket and the podium. Points are punched as dot-matrix holes.

08 · teamDisc()

Ornament team discs

A colour ball with a brass cap and loop and a navy mascot glyph. The leader wears a gold star topper (the crown is retired), and the team that is up wears the pulsing red-nose beacon. Teams 7 and 8 have colour-blind lookalikes, so they are offered last.

09 · .score-strip · .ribbon

Score strip

A frosted bar, the only glass besides toasts. The active tile scales to 1.06 with a tinsel border, a gold glow, the cranberry UP NOW ribbon with candy-stripe ends and the red-nose beacon. Points count up over 700 ms and the tile flashes its team colour once.

10 · .marquee · .letterboard · .bulb-string

Now showing

A cranberry marquee ringed by 28 chasing bulbs, with letters dropping onto the ivory letterboard one every 40 ms. The bulb string has steady, chase, flash, off, plug-in (flicker, die, blaze) and multicolour C9 states.

11 · timerRing()

Timer rings

A gold arc on a 12% snow track with a glowing bulb at its head and the number in Figtree 800 tabular. Under 5 s the arc turns berry-hi.

12 · .splash

Splash

A full-bleed slot colour with a monoline wrapping-paper repeat at 9%, and the word in Fraunces 900 WONK. It pops in from 0.6 to 1.08 to 1 over 400 ms.

13 · .letterbox · toast() · showBanner()

Letterbox, toasts & the reconnect banner

Letterbox bars slide in to 2.39:1 over 400 ms for Double Down and Grinch Steal. Toasts are frosted pills with a gold rim, and the reconnect banner is a gold marquee strip.

14 · phone controller

On the phone

The phone stays dark with the same glows. The header is a ticket stub tinted with the team colour. Each screen has one job, and after any submit the phone points people to the TV.

15 · sound.js

Sound board

Sleigh bells and celesta. Unlock audio first (top right). Every effect is a no-op before unlock or while muted.
